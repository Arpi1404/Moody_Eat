# MoodyEat — 6-Week Launch Playbook

_For two founders who'd rather build than post. Everything here is async:
writing, scheduling, and sending links. No camera-facing content required.
Written 15 July 2026. Companion files: `captions.md`, `reddit-post.md`,
`influencer-dm.md`._

**The one rule:** judge yourself on Plausible (`visits → quest_created →
quest_shared`) and share-link views — never on follower count.

**Weekly rhythm (repeat every week, ~2–3 hours total):**
- One 60–90 min "content sitting": queue the week's posts in Meta Business
  Suite (business.facebook.com — free scheduler, works for IG).
- One 15-min Plausible review (Sunday): are completed quests being shared?
- Reply to every comment/DM within 24h (usually minutes of typing).

---

## Week 1 — The friend test + fill the grid

Goal: find out if strangers understand the product, and stop looking like an
empty account.

1. **Friend test (Day 1, 10 minutes).** Send 5 real people a quest link on
   WhatsApp. Watch: does the preview render? Do they open it? Do they get it
   without you explaining? If this flops, fix the share moment before
   spending another minute on content.
2. **Generate the asset pack.** Run the dev server + prod proxy, open
   `/dev/marketing-cards`, hit "Download all". You now have 24 PNGs
   (12 story + 12 feed).
3. **Post the first 9 grid posts** over Week 1–2 (queue them all in one
   sitting; captions in `captions.md`): 4 curated quest cards, 1 "how it
   works" carousel (screenshots of build → share), 1 founder/why post
   (text-on-brand-background, no faces needed), 3 more quest cards.
4. **Set up profile finishing touches:** pin the "how it works" post,
   create two Highlights ("How it works", "Quests").

## Week 2 — First reels + Reddit

Goal: first algorithmic reach.

1. **Record 2 screen-recording reels** (phone screen capture, no face, no
   voice — add trending audio + captions in the IG editor):
   - Reel A: "Date night in Banjara Hills, planned in 30 seconds" — record
     picking mood → budget → quest appears → map.
   - Reel B: "POV: nobody in the group can decide where to go" — record the
     share link landing in WhatsApp and opening.
   Scripts + hooks in `captions.md`. 30–40 seconds max, hook in first 2s.
2. **Post the r/hyderabad launch post** (`reddit-post.md`). Pick a weekday
   evening. Reply to every comment for 48 hours — this is typing, not
   socializing, and it's your single biggest traffic day this month.
3. Keep cadence: 2 reels + 1 carousel this week.

## Week 3 — Influencer seeding round 1

Goal: borrowed audiences.

1. **Shortlist 10 Hyderabad food/lifestyle pages** (10k–50k followers —
   search #hyderabadfoodie, #hyderabaddiaries and note who posts reels of
   cafes/street food).
2. **Send the DM** (`influencer-dm.md`): offer a quest with their name on
   it — built at moodyeat.in/create with the "Made by" field, shared as
   their own link. Costs you nothing; takes them 3 minutes.
3. For each yes: build the quest together over DM, they post the story
   card, you repost. Their name is on the quest page every time it's shared.
4. Keep cadence: 2 reels + 1 carousel (start reusing formats that worked).

## Week 4 — Double down on what worked

Goal: stop guessing, start repeating.

1. Open IG insights + Plausible side by side. Rank every post/reel by
   *link clicks* (not likes). Make 3 more of whatever won.
2. **UGC flywheel:** anyone who shares a story card and tags you gets
   reshared to your story same-day. Make it feel guaranteed.
3. Second Reddit-style post in 2–3 Hyderabad foodie Facebook/WhatsApp
   communities (adapt `reddit-post.md`; one post each, never repeat-spam).
4. Keep cadence.

## Week 5 — Influencer round 2 + WhatsApp seeding

1. 10 more influencer DMs (include a screenshot of a round-1 named quest —
   social proof makes round 2 convert better).
2. **Personal-network WhatsApp push:** each founder sends the app to 3
   group chats with a real quest link for that group ("Friday plan sorted
   👇"). The link IS the demo.
3. Keep cadence.

## Week 6 — Review honestly

Sit down with Plausible + the quest-store view counts and answer the
ROADMAP's questions:

- **Shares per completed quest ≥ 10%?** If lower → the share moment needs
  work (card, CTA placement), not more reach.
- **Visits high but quest_created low?** → landing/builder friction.
- **Created high but quest_started low?** → plan credibility (place
  quality, durations).
- **Which channel drove actual quest creation?** Kill what didn't, double
  what did, and set the next 6 weeks accordingly.

If the numbers are good: this is when scheduling `verify_curated.py`,
adding streaks, and "Free right now" mode earn their place. If they're
bad: fix product, don't buy reach.

---

## What NOT to do (it will feel tempting)

- No engagement bots, follower buying, or auto-DM tools — Instagram
  shadow-buries accounts for this, and it's unrecoverable.
- No paid ads until the organic share loop proves out (ROADMAP gate).
- No second city, no accounts feature — same gates as before.
- Don't judge before Week 6. Reels reach is lumpy; consistency wins.
