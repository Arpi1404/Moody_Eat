# Micro-influencer DM template

_Target: Hyderabad food/lifestyle pages, 10k–50k followers (big enough to
have reach, small enough to reply). Find them via #hyderabadfoodie /
#hyderabaddiaries reels. Send from @moody_eat. Personalize the first line
every time — one specific detail from their content, or don't send it._

---

## The DM (round 1)

> Hey [name]! Your reel on [specific place/post — be real] was exactly the
> kind of spot-hunting we're into.
>
> We're two Hyderabad builders behind moodyeat.in — it plans a full evening
> out (2–4 stops, mapped, timed, budgeted) from one tap. Free, no signup.
>
> We'd love to make **a quest with your name on it** — like "[Name]'s Old
> City Crawl": your 3–4 favourite spots, built into a proper timed route
> at moodyeat.in/create with "Made by [name]" on the page and on the story
> card. Takes 3 minutes, costs nothing, and every time anyone shares it,
> your name travels with it.
>
> If you're in, just send me your 3–4 spots and I'll build it and send you
> the link + a ready-to-post story card. No obligations either way — and
> if you ever want it taken down, one message.

## Follow-up (if no reply after 4–5 days, once only)

> No stress if this isn't your thing! One-liner version: your favourite
> spots → a named, shareable evening plan at moodyeat.in, free, 3 minutes.
> If yes, just reply with the spots 🙌

## When they say yes

1. Get their 3–4 spots (and rough order if they care).
2. Build it at moodyeat.in/create — "Made by [their name/handle]".
3. Share → Copy link + Download PNG. Send them both.
4. Ask them to post the story card with the link sticker; repost their
   story from @moody_eat the same day.
5. Log it: date, handle, quest link — check its `/stats` view count a week
   later so you know which creators actually move the needle.

## Round 2 upgrade (week 5)

Same DM, plus a screenshot of a round-1 named quest and one line:
> We did this with [handle] — their quest's been opened [N] times.

---

_Rules: max 10 DMs per round, spread over 2–3 days (don't trip spam
filters). Never automate the sending. Never offer money at this stage —
the named quest IS the offer, and it filters for people who genuinely
like the product._
